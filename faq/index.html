    Frequently Asked Questions

    Quick answers to common homeowner questions.

    When is trash and recycling pickup?

    Trash is collected Tuesday; recycling every other Tuesday. Carts may be at the curb from 6:00 PM the evening before and must be put away by the end of pickup day.

    Can I park on the street overnight?

    Short-term guest parking is allowed. Vehicles may not be parked on the street for more than 72 consecutive hours.

    Do I need approval to paint my front door?

    Yes, if you’re changing the color. Same-color repainting does not require approval. See Architectural Requests.

    How do I report a streetlight outage or common-area issue?

    Email the board or the management company using the Contact page. Include the nearest address or a photo.

    When and where are board meetings?

    The second Tuesday of every month at 7:00 PM in the clubhouse. Agendas are posted under News.

    How do I get a pool pass?

    Submit the pool pass application on the Documents page. Your dues must be current.
