Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common homeowner questions.

When is trash and recycling pickup?

Trash is collected Thursday; recycling every other Thursday. Carts should be put away within 48 hours of a pickup. Scheduled pickups of bulk items scheduled with Durham city may remain at the curb until such time as the city picks them up

Can I park on the street overnight?

Short-term guest parking is allowed. Per the covenants of the neighborhood, all vehicles should be parked on an appropriate parking pad on the homeowner’s property and in such a way as to not impede the sidewalks. With that in mind, since all streets in the Autumn Ridge community are public roadways, anyone may technically park on them; however, it is not in the spirit of what was agreed to buy all homeowners when purchasing property in the neighborhood

Do I need approval to paint my front door?

Yes, if you’re changing the color. Same-color repainting does not require approval. See Architectural Requests.

How do I report a streetlight outage or common-area issue?

The City of Durham is responsible for street lights. The main portal into city services is Durham One Call. You can call: 919-560-1200, text: 919-813-6315, submit a request online Durham One Call Online Submission, or download the mobile application Apple Android

When and where are board meetings?

Board meetings are held as needed. To submit an item for the board’s consideration, contact the property management company, which will forward it to the board for discussion when appropriate. See Contact
