Autumn Ridge is a community of 142 single-family homes. The HOA maintains our entrance, common areas, and works to keep the neighborhood a great place to live.

Board meetings are held ad hoc and Annual Home Owner’s Association Meetings are held every year in September with notices sent 30 days in advance by our management company
