Architectural Requests

How to get approval for exterior changes to your home.

Most exterior changes require written approval from the Architectural Review Committee (ARC) before work begins.

What Needs Approval

  • Fences, sheds, decks, patios, and pergolas
  • Exterior paint color changes, roofing, siding, windows, and doors
  • Major landscaping changes, tree removal, and hardscaping
  • Solar panels, generators, and pools/hot tubs

Routine maintenance using the same colors and materials usually does not need approval.

How to Submit

  1. Review the Architectural Guidelines from the Documents page for official, detailed guidelines. Direct Link
  2. Download the Architectural Request Form from the Documents page. Direct Link
  3. Attach a site plan, photos, paint samples, or product specs as applicable.
  4. Email the completed packet to the board (see Contact).
  5. The ARC responds within 30 days. Approval is required before starting work.

IMPORTANT NOTE If your project requires a permit, be sure to obtain all necessary approvals from the city, county, state, or other relevant authority before moving forward. HOA approval does not replace or waive any required permits or compliance with local regulations.
