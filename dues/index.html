Important Payment Policies

Automated Payments

Homeowners are responsible for canceling any recurring or automated payment methods, including:

  • Bank bill-pay services
  • Credit card payments
  • Electronic checks
  • Any other scheduled payment methods

This responsibility also applies when a property is being sold or is scheduled to close.

Note: Submitting a closing request does not automatically stop recurring payments.

Account Identification

William Douglas Management is not responsible for:

  • Paper checks submitted without a homeowner account number
  • Online bill-pay checks that do not include a homeowner account number

Including your account number helps ensure prompt and accurate processing.

Returned Payments

Any payment returned by a bank, for reasons other than an error by William Douglas Management, may result in a returned item fee being charged to your account.

Refunds

Refunds over $50 are subject to a refund processing fee.

Mailed Check and Stop-Payment Policy

Please Read Carefully

All checks mailed to William Douglas Management are sent directly to a lockbox processing center.

Because of this process:

  • William Douglas Management cannot stop a mailed check from being deposited.
  • The lockbox is managed by a third-party processing center.
  • The processing center does not have access to homeowner account information.
  • All checks received at the lockbox are scanned and posted automatically.

If a homeowner requests a stop payment after a check has been mailed, the homeowner is responsible for any fees associated with that stop payment.

Recommendation

William Douglas Management advises homeowners not to issue stop payments on checks that have already been mailed.
